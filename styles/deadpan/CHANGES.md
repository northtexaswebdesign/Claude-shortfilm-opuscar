# Changes from Urban Sketch · Pen & Wash

| Section | Base (urban-sketch) | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| §1 Essence | a travel sketchbook page drawn on location | a single-panel magazine cartoon: people, attitude, one caption | the user's New Yorker Cartoon, Ink and Wash prompt |
| §2 Ink | near-constant 0.3–0.5 mm sepia fineliner | warm-black nib line, width swells 1/1000 → 1/300 of frame width | the prompt's nib; reads as hand-inked cartoon |
| §2 Paper | cream cold-press, painting frays at the page edge | paper `#FBF8F1`, soft fade to white at the frame edge | the prompt's paper; the cartoon fills the page |
| §2 People | 3D skeleton figures, face left blank | 2D cartoon rig (`toon.js`): big heads and noses, half-lidded eyes, brows, talking hands, hair styles, six ready characters | the prompt's "drawn expressive faces"; the base figures were stiff and had no faces |
| §3 Colour | few pigments, local colour, violet shadows, example palettes of 3–4 greens/blues | exactly four pale washes (grey, sea blue, sand, rust) | the prompt's palette; stops the page going saturated |
| §4 Type | handwriting on the page, corner notes, margin subtitles | one hand-lettered caption (Patrick Hand SC, about 1/30 frame height) written letter by letter; other text in Caveat / Reenie Beanie | the prompt's caption; one joke, one line |
| §5 Motion | figures boil at 12 fps, page static, invisible forces drawn | same boil rule; acting is small (blink, tilt, one hand), no bounce; page turns wash out to paper | keeps the signature; adds the deadpan restraint |
| §6 Camera | travel across a large page, crane, whip-tilt, punch-in | slow push, sideways drift, hold-then-push on the punchline, pull back, tilt into the caption; no whips | cartoons hold still |
| §7 Sound | sketchbook ambience, wet brush, many music families | paper room tone, pen and caption scratch, office/living-room foley at cartoon scale; jazz trio, guitar, ukulele, accordion | the prompt's jazz references; quieter palette |
| §8 Native moves | line before colour, colour as arrival, the one coloured thing, page as frame, carrot people | the caption lands, the straight man, the deadpan turn, the cutaway page (line before colour kept by the engine) | the style's own signatures |
| §9 Pitfalls | colour-field clipping, occlusion of far ink, IK feet | heavy outlines, ears that read as eyes, joint seams on limbs, clone faces, last letter clipped, over-complete rooms | what went wrong while building the first six films |
